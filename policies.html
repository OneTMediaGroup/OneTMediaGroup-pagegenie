Build something useful and respectful. These rules apply to prompts, generated output, text edits, uploads, logos, links, embeds and published sites.

  • No targeted harassment, threats, bullying, stalking, doxxing or publishing private information without lawful authority.
  • No child sexual exploitation, sexualisation of minors, non-consensual intimate content or sexual exploitation. PageGenie does not host pornography.
